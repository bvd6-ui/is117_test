# One White T-Shirt, Many Meanings

## A Short Guide to Persuasion, Branding, Design, and AI-Assisted Work

How can an ordinary white T-shirt become an invitation to explore, a symbol of independence, or a reassuring everyday choice without changing its fabric? This mini textbook uses that question to introduce three connected lenses: persuasion, brand archetypes, and visual design language.

Written for first-year college students, the guide moves from understanding creative choices to directing AI-assisted work through clear specifications, useful checks, version control, and human judgment. The recurring campaigns are hypothetical examples, not claims about actual brands.

## How to Use This Guide

Read the chapters in order, then return to the tables and questions when developing a brief of your own. Compare how each T-shirt presentation changes the meaning while keeping product facts fixed. Treat the diagrams as discussion tools, not formulas that guarantee results.

Follow the museum research instructions in Chapter 3 to investigate authentic objects and verify their records. Distinguish sourced historical claims from hypothetical examples and your own interpretations. At the end, use Chapter 5 to plan a small AI-assisted task and decide which checks and human review it needs.

For the repository exercise, review the Markdown and Mermaid diagrams before committing your work. Automated checks can help with explicit requirements; you remain responsible for evidence, meaning, and final decisions.
